Topic: why your pool turns green after a storm

1. What problem does your customer have that this video helps with?

Um, okay so the big one this time of year is people call me and they're like, my pool was fine on Sunday and now it's green, what happened. And it's always right after a storm. The monsoon comes through, the power goes out for a couple hours, everybody's out dealing with the yard and the trees and whatever, and nobody's thinking about the pool. And then two, three days later it's green. And they think they messed up the chemicals, and usually they didn't, usually the pump just wasn't running when it should've been, or it was running and nothing was getting through.

So I go look at the timer, it's one of the old dial ones, the gray box on the side of the house, and the dial's off by, it was five hours, no wait, more like four and a half, because when the power goes out those old dial timers just stop, and when it comes back on they pick up right where they left off. So nothing's running when it's supposed to. And then the skimmer basket was packed, I mean packed solid with mesquite pods from the storm, so even when it did run barely anything was getting through.
