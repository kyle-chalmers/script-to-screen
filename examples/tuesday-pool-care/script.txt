Why your pool turns green after a storm

Take 1
Hey, if your pool turned green right after a storm, you probably didn't do anything wrong. The monsoon comes through, the power goes out for a couple hours, and nobody's thinking about the pool. Two, three days later it's green.

Take 2
Last August, big storm on a Saturday night. I get there Tuesday, and I mean it was pea soup, I couldn't see the bottom step. And this lady's standing there like, I didn't touch anything. And she didn't!

Take 3
Her timer, the gray box on the side of the house, was off by four and a half hours. When the power goes out, those old dial timers just stop. And the skimmer basket was packed solid with mesquite pods.

Take 4
So the morning after a storm, if the time on the dial doesn't match your phone, turn it the way the little arrow points till it does. Then dump the skimmer basket. That's it. Takes five minutes.

Take 5
And if it's already green, just call me, or call your pool guy, whoever.
