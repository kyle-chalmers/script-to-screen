Topic: why your pool turns green after a storm

Who it's for: "people call me and they're like, my pool was fine on Sunday and now it's green, what happened."

Hook (your opening line, word for word):
"hey, if your pool turned green right after a storm, you probably didn't do anything wrong."

Real moment (word for word):
"I get there Tuesday, that's her day, and the water is, I mean it was pea soup, I couldn't see the bottom step. And she's standing there like, I didn't touch anything. And she didn't! So I go look at the timer, it's one of the old dial ones, the gray box on the side of the house, and the dial's off by, it was five hours, no wait, more like four and a half"

What to do after: The morning after a storm, check that the time on the timer dial matches your phone, and turn it the way the little arrow points until it does. Pull the lid off the skimmer and dump the basket. If the pool is already green, don't add a bunch of chemicals; call me or your pool guy.

Order:
1. The opening line: you probably didn't do anything wrong
2. The real moment: pea soup, and the timer off by four and a half hours
3. What to do: check the timer, dump the skimmer basket
4. The ask: if it's already green, call me

Words I never say: algae bloom, chemical imbalance, crystal clear
